Customer Data Policy v1

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party vendors who access, process, or store customer data within the organization's systems.

2. Data Classification
Customer data is classified into three categories:
- Public: Information approved for public release (e.g., marketing materials, published reports)
- Internal: Operational data not intended for public release (e.g., internal procedures, training materials)
- Confidential: Sensitive customer information including names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, and account numbers

3. Data Retention
Customer records must be retained for five years from the date of account closure. After the retention period, records must be securely destroyed using approved data destruction methods.

4. Access Control
Access to confidential customer data is restricted to authorized personnel only. Access requests must be submitted through the IT service desk and approved by the data owner. Access logs must be reviewed monthly.

5. Data Breach Response
In the event of a suspected data breach, the incident must be reported to the security team within 24 hours. The security team will coordinate with legal and compliance teams to assess the impact and notify affected customers within 72 hours.
