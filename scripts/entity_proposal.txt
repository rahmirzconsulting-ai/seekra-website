Proposal — Entity Relationship Layer (“Knowledge Graph Lite”)
for Seekra
Status: For architect review → implementation brief Date: 2026-08-17 Author: Platform review
(post Brief #15 verification) Scope discipline: This proposal deliberately avoids a graph database and
LLM-based relationship extraction. It builds on verified, already-deployed components.
