Why Seekra Exists

Three problems every large archive suffers from

01
folder_off
Knowledge buried in files
Critical information lives inside thousands of PDFs, scans, and Office documents that no one can effectively search. Staff spend hours locating a single clause, and institutional knowledge walks out the door when experienced employees leave.
02
cloud_off
Cloud AI means data leaves the building
Mainstream AI assistants ask you to upload your documents to their servers. For government, legal, healthcare, and financial work, that is often non-negotiable — the data cannot leave your jurisdiction or your network.
03
help_outline
Answers without sources can’t be trusted
When an AI produces an answer with no link to where the information came from, it cannot be verified. In regulated environments, an unverifiable answer is the same as no answer at all.
