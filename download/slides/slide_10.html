DEPLOYMENT

Three tiers. One platform. You choose

Seekra runs in three deployment tiers — from cloud-native to fully air-gapped — so you choose exactly where the AI runs and how isolated your environment is. The platform stays the same; only the AI location and network exposure change.

cloud
Cloud Native

Seekra runs in your cloud tenant and can leverage industry-leading external AI models — ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude — with PII automatically masked before any external call. Best AI capability, fastest to deploy.

TIER 01 · FASTEST

Self-Hosted

Seekra runs entirely on your own infrastructure — cloud VM or on-premises servers — with AI models hosted locally. Full data sovereignty. Nothing leaves your network.

Air-Gapped

Fully isolated self-hosted deployment with no external network connection. For classified, sovereign, or maximum-security workloads where even update traffic is not permitted.

• Three deployment tiers, one platform — the same Seekra application, PII masking, and document store across all tiers; only the AI location and network exposure change. • Tier 01 Cloud Native: fastest to deploy, leverages best-in-class external AI models with PII masking as the protection layer. Good for evaluation and for organizations not ready to host AI locally. • Tier 02 Self-Hosted: full data sovereignty, AI runs locally on your infrastructure (cloud VM or on-prem), no AI traffic leaves your network. • Tier 03 Air-Gapped: maximum security for classified/sovereign workloads. No external network at all — updates via physical media or data diode. • Most clients start with Cloud Native for evaluation, then upgrade to Self-Hosted or Air-Gapped for production as their sovereignty requirements mature. • Pricing is always discussed in person during the demo, never published.
