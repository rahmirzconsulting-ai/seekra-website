DEPLOYMENT

Three ways to run Seekra. You choose

Seekra adapts to your infrastructure policy. Move between deployment models as your needs evolve — the platform stays the same.

cloud
Cloud VM

Runs on your own AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud account. You control the keys, the region, and the network. Data stays inside your tenant.

STANDARD
shield_lock
Air-gapped / offline

Fully isolated. No external network connection required. The strongest possible deployment for classified, regulated, or sovereign workloads.

FEATURED · MOST SECURE
storage
On-premises

Installed on servers inside your data center. No traffic to any external cloud. Operates inside your existing security perimeter.

No pricing is published. Deployment model and scale are discussed in person during the demo.
• Three deployment options, one platform — migration between models is straightforward. • Most clients start with Cloud VM for evaluation, then move to On-premises or Air-gapped for production. • Air-gapped is the differentiator for Gulf government — emphasize this is real today, not a roadmap item. • Pricing is always discussed in person during the demo, never published.
