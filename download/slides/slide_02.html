Executive Summary

One platform. Your entire archive. Nothing leaves the building.

Seekra is a content-aware intelligence platform that runs entirely on the customer’s own infrastructure. Organizations upload their documents — contracts, policies, case files, scanned images, field photographs — and Seekra lets their staff ask questions in natural language, search by image, and speak to the archive in Arabic, Urdu, or English.

Every answer carries numbered citations that link directly to the exact source page. Personal information is automatically masked before any AI model is involved. Role-based access controls and a complete audit trail make Seekra suitable for the most regulated Gulf and Pakistani environments — including fully air-gapped deployments where no external network connection is permitted.
