## POST 1 (Article — ~400 words)

**Headline: Why "self-hosted AI" is not the same as "AI that respects your data"**

Most enterprise AI tools today fall into one of two camps:

**Camp 1: Cloud AI assistants.** ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini. Excellent generalists. But they ask you to upload your documents to their servers. For a law firm handling client contracts, a hospital with patient records, or a government entity with classified files — that's a non-starter. The data cannot leave your jurisdiction or your network. Period.

**Camp 2: "Self-hosted" AI tools.** They run on your infrastructure. Your data stays on your servers. But here's what most of them miss: they don't actually prove it.

They say "your data never leaves your servers" — but can they prove that PII was masked before the LLM call? Can they show you an audit trail that hasn't been tampered with? Can they trace a specific answer back to the exact chunk of the exact document it came from?

At Seekra, we built something different. Three layers of provable governance:

1. **Tamper-evident audit trail** — every action is hash-chained. If anyone — even a database administrator — retroactively modifies an audit entry, the chain breaks. You can verify the chain at any time via the admin API.

2. **PII lineage tracking** — when personal information is detected and masked before an LLM call, the event is logged. You can prove, cryptographically, that an email address was masked before being sent to any external model. The regulator asks "was this patient email ever sent to OpenAI in raw form?" — Seekra answers with evidence, not assertions.

3. **Provenance graph** — every answer is traceable to its source chunk, its PII findings, and the LLM calls that received the masked version. One API call shows the full lineage.

This isn't "self-hosted AI." This is **provable AI governance** — and no platform in the Gulf enterprise AI segment offers this combination.

We built Seekra for Gulf government, legal, healthcare, and financial buyers who need more than a verbal promise. They need proof.
