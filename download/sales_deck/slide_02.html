The Problem

Your documents are buried.

01

Knowledge locked in files

Thousands of PDFs, scans, and Office documents nobody can effectively search. Staff spend hours locating a single clause.

02

Cloud AI takes your data

Mainstream AI assistants ask you to upload documents to their servers. For government, legal, healthcare — that's non-negotiable.

03

Answers without proof

When an AI produces an answer with no link to its source, it can't be verified. In regulated environments, that's the same as no answer.
